Household Finance Tracker

A read-only personal budgeting and debt-repayment tool used solely by its household owners.

Purpose

The application brings the owners’ account balances and transactions into a local evidence ledger so they can reconcile household spending and plan debt repayments.

It does not initiate payments, provide banking services to the public, or sell financial data.
