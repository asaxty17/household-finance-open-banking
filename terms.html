Terms of use

Private use only

Household Finance Tracker is a private, non-commercial application used only by its household owners to review their own finances. It is not a public financial product or service.

Read-only access

The application requests account-information access for balances and transactions. It does not provide or implement payment initiation. Any debt repayment remains a separate action performed and authorised by the account holder through their bank or creditor.

No financial advice

Calculations, forecasts and repayment scenarios are informational planning aids. They are not regulated financial, legal, tax or debt advice and do not guarantee savings, repayment dates or future account balances.

Accuracy and availability

Bank data can be delayed, incomplete, duplicated, corrected or temporarily unavailable. Account holders should verify balances, payment dates, interest rates and repayment instructions directly with the relevant bank or creditor before acting.

Account-holder responsibilities

Account holders are responsible for granting access only to accounts they are authorised to share, protecting their devices and banking credentials, reviewing imported information, and maintaining sufficient funds for required payments.

Consent and termination

Open Banking consent is voluntary and can be revoked through the relevant bank or Enable Banking. Use of the connection may end whenever consent expires, is revoked or the application is discontinued.

Changes

These terms may be updated when the application’s scope or data handling changes. The effective date shown on this page identifies the current version.
