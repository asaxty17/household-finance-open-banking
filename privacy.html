Privacy notice

About this application

Household Finance Tracker is a private, non-commercial tool used solely by its household owners. It is not offered to customers or the public.

Information used

With an account holder’s explicit Open Banking consent, the application may retrieve account details, account-holder names, balances, transactions, product information and consent or synchronisation metadata from the account holder’s selected financial institution.

The application does not receive or store online-banking passwords, security codes or multi-factor authentication credentials.

Why information is used

Information is used only for personal household budgeting, transaction reconciliation, cash-flow forecasting, debt-repayment planning and producing private reports for the account holders.

Storage and security

Financial records are held locally on an owner-controlled computer. Open Banking signing keys and reusable session credentials are kept outside the finance database in protected local storage. Financial records are not published on this policy website.

Sharing

Information is not sold, used for advertising or shared with unrelated third parties. Enable Banking and the selected bank process information only as necessary to establish and operate the account-information connection.

Retention and control

Records are retained only for the household’s budgeting and repayment history. An account holder can revoke Open Banking access through their bank or Enable Banking and can ask the application owner to delete locally held records relating to them.

Payments and automated decisions

The application uses read-only account-information access. It cannot initiate payments and does not make automated decisions with legal or similarly significant effects.

Cookies and analytics

This policy website uses no application cookies, analytics or advertising trackers. GitHub Pages may process basic technical request data under GitHub’s own policies.

Contact

Privacy enquiries can be sent to the application owner using the verified data-protection contact supplied in the Enable Banking consent information.
